## 2. About BDOPS

### Section Title

**A Community Built Around Technology**

### Content

BDOPS, Binary Development Operations, is a technology community created as a space for people who want to grow through technology.

We connect people from different technology backgrounds and encourage continuous learning, knowledge sharing, collaboration, experimentation, and real project experience.

BDOPS is not limited to one specific technology field. The community is designed to bring together people with different skills, perspectives, and interests while sharing the same goal: learning, building, and growing together.

The website should follow the same visual identity as the current BDOPS Instagram feed:

- Deep navy background
- Electric blue highlights
- White typography
- Soft blue glow
- Clean technology inspired layout
- Large typography
- Minimal decorative elements
- BDOPS logo used as a subtle background element
- BEDE used selectively as a supporting character
- Consistent rounded cards for information blocks

## Tone of Voice

The communication style should be:

- Professional
- Modern
- Technology focused
- Community oriented
- Clear
- Confident
- Welcoming
- Not overly corporate
- Not overly casual
